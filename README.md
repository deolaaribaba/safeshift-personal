# SafeShift 🚨

> For carers — because your life matters too.

## Why I Built This
I have seen it. Carers work 40–45+ hours a week, push past limits, ignore their bodies — and some do not make it home.

This app is a lifeline. It tracks your hours, sounds the alarm BEFORE it’s too late, and sends a warning to YOU and MANAGEMENT when the workload is unsafe.

## What It Does
- ✅ Tracks your weekly hours
- ✅ Warns you when limits are near ⚠️
- ✅ Sends ALERT to carer AND management when unsafe 🚨
- ✅ Shows your hours history
- ✅ Simple — works on any phone or computer

## Built With
HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript · VS Code
